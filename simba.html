CASE STUDY 03 • SIMBA

Building a Repeatable Content Growth System Around Simba

A personal content-growth project focused on building a repeatable system for content planning, publishing, distribution, analytics and future monetization.

PROJECT

MR SIMBA

FOCUS

Content Growth

CHANNELS

YouTube • Facebook

ROLE

Content Strategy & Growth Practice

01 • PROBLEM

Content Ideas Alone Do Not Create a Growth System.

A dog-content channel can easily become a collection of random videos. Without a clear audience, content pillars, publishing rhythm and measurement system, it becomes difficult to understand what is actually working.

The goal of this project is therefore not simply to upload more videos, but to build a repeatable content operating system.

02 • AUDIENCE

Start With People, Not Just the Dog.

The content needs to understand why people would choose to watch Simba repeatedly.

Dog Lovers

People who enjoy watching everyday Labrador behaviour and personality.

Pet Owners

People interested in relatable experiences, routines and dog-care content.

Entertainment Viewers

People looking for funny, emotional or satisfying short-form content.

Labrador Audience

Viewers specifically interested in Labrador behaviour and lifestyle.

03 • CONTENT PILLARS

Build Repeatable Content Categories.

Personality

Simba's expressions, habits, reactions and unique behaviour.

Daily Life

Sleeping, walking, eating, playing and everyday routines.

Food

Suitable food-related content and reactions, presented responsibly.

Commands & Training

Simple commands, learning moments and behaviour-based content.

Family Moments

Natural interactions between Simba and family members.

Growth Experiments

Testing hooks, formats, topics and presentation styles.

04 • CONTENT SYSTEM

One Idea Can Become Multiple Formats.

Content Idea
→
Short Video
→
Long Video
→
Social Post
→
Community

The objective is to reduce the pressure of creating completely new ideas every day by building multiple content outputs from strong concepts.

05 • PUBLISHING

Consistency Through a Repeatable Workflow.

01 Idea

Select an idea from the content system.

02 Capture

Record the natural moment or planned video.

03 Edit

Create the appropriate short or long format.

04 Publish

Publish using the platform-specific format.

05 Review

Study performance and improve the next piece.

06 • DISTRIBUTION

One Content System, Multiple Platforms.

YouTube

Shorts and longer videos designed around viewer retention and discovery.

Facebook

Repurpose suitable content for another audience and distribution channel.

Cross-Platform

Adapt the same core idea instead of blindly duplicating every upload.

Audience Feedback

Comments, shares, saves and viewer reactions help identify future content opportunities.

07 • ANALYTICS

Measure What Helps the Next Decision.

Reach

Views and impressions indicate how widely content was discovered.

Retention

Watch time and audience retention help evaluate whether the content holds attention.

Engagement

Likes, comments, shares and saves provide audience feedback.

Conversion

Profile actions, subscribers and other desired actions help evaluate deeper audience interest.

08 • EXPERIMENTATION

Treat Content Like a Growth Experiment.

Hypothesis
→
Create
→
Publish
→
Measure
→
Learn

Instead of assuming that a topic will work, the system uses actual audience response to decide what to improve, repeat or stop.

09 • FUTURE MONETIZATION

Build the Audience Before Expanding Monetization.

The first priority is building a consistent audience and understanding which content creates meaningful engagement.

Platform Monetization

Explore eligible platform monetization as the channel develops.

Brand Opportunities

Relevant pet brands could become a future partnership opportunity if audience fit develops.

Affiliate Opportunities

Relevant products may eventually provide additional monetization possibilities.

Content Portfolio

The project also demonstrates practical skills in content strategy, analytics and growth systems.

10 • LEARNING

Consistency Becomes Easier When Content Is a System.

The goal is not to find one viral video. The goal is to build a system that can repeatedly create, distribute, measure and improve content.

Simba gives me a practical environment to test content strategy, audience thinking, publishing systems, analytics and optimization without pretending that unproven results are guaranteed.
