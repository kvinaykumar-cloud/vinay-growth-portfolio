CASE STUDY 02 • RAKSHANA INTERIORS

Turning Completed Interior Projects Into Growth Assets

A real-world growth project focused on using existing interior design project proof, digital presence, content and customer-focused lead generation.

BUSINESS

Rakshana Interiors

FOCUS

Business Growth

ASSET

10 Completed Project Videos

ROLE

Growth & Marketing Practice

01 • CONTEXT

The Business Already Had Proof.

Rakshana Interiors already had completed interior design projects and project videos. The opportunity was to turn that existing work into a stronger digital marketing asset.

Instead of treating each project video as just another social media upload, I looked at how the projects could communicate quality, trust and business value to potential customers.

02 • CUSTOMER

What Does an Interior Customer Need to Believe?

Interior customers are not simply buying a video, design image or furniture item. They are making a significant home investment and need confidence before contacting a business.

Quality

Does the finished work look professional and suitable for the customer's expectations?

Trust

Can this business actually complete the project as promised?

Materials

What materials, finishes and products are being used?

Cost

What factors influence the project budget?

Process

How does the project move from planning to completion?

After-Service

What happens if the customer needs support after completion?

03 • STRATEGY

Turn Project Proof Into a Customer Journey.

The strategy was to connect completed projects with the questions customers ask before choosing an interior design company.

Completed Project
→
Content
→
Trust
→
Enquiry
→
Consultation

04 • CONTENT SYSTEM

One Completed Project Can Create Multiple Content Assets.

Project Showcase

Show the finished interior and communicate the transformation.

Before & After

Show the difference between the original space and completed project.

Materials

Explain important material and finish choices that customers may want to understand.

Process

Show selected stages of planning, execution and completion.

Customer Questions

Create content around common questions about cost, timelines, materials and service.

Trust Content

Use completed work and customer experiences as proof of capability.

05 • LEAD GENERATION

Move From Inspiration to Enquiry.

Interior content should not stop at getting views. The next step is giving interested customers a clear path to ask about their own project.

01 Discovery

Customer discovers a project or useful content.

02 Interest

Customer explores more projects and information.

03 Trust

Project proof reduces uncertainty.

04 Enquiry

Customer contacts the business.

05 Consultation

Requirement and project details are discussed.

06 • MEASUREMENT

What I Would Measure.

Content Performance

Reach, views, watch time, engagement and profile visits.

Intent

Saves, shares, comments, profile actions and direct enquiries.

Lead Quality

Project type, location, requirements, budget and timeline.

Business Outcome

Qualified consultations, proposals and eventually completed projects.

07 • MY ROLE

What I Actually Worked On.

My role in this project is focused on growth and marketing thinking. I am not presenting myself as the interior designer or project execution team.

Growth Thinking

Studied how the existing business proof could support customer acquisition.

Content Strategy

Planned how completed projects could be presented around customer needs.

Lead Generation

Designed the pathway from content discovery toward customer enquiry.

Portfolio Documentation

Used the project as a practical environment to apply and document growth concepts.

08 • LEARNING

Existing Business Assets Can Become Growth Assets.

A completed project is not only proof of past work. With the right positioning, it can become content, trust, education and a starting point for new customer conversations.

This project helped me understand how a local service business can connect its existing work with digital content, customer psychology and lead generation.
